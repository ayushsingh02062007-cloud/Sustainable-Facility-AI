# 🌱 Sustainable Facility AI

## AI-Powered Sustainable Facility & Estate Intelligence Dashboard

Sustainable Facility AI is an AI-powered facility intelligence platform combining Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, IoT analytics, predictive intelligence, real-time dashboards, and 3D Digital Twin technology.

## 🚀 Features

- Intelligent Facility Dashboard
- Energy Monitoring & Prediction
- Water Monitoring & Prediction
- Waste Monitoring & Prediction
- Air Quality Intelligence
- Traffic Intelligence
- Asset Intelligence
- Facility Risk Assessment
- AI Anomaly Detection
- AI Recommendations
- Forecasting
- 3D Digital Twin
- Sustainability Intelligence
- AI-powered Decision Support

## 🧠 AI / ML

| Model | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Random Forest Regressor | Energy Prediction |
| Random Forest Regressor | Water Prediction |
| Random Forest Regressor | Waste Prediction |
| Random Forest Regressor | Risk Prediction |
| Isolation Forest | Anomaly Detection |

## 📊 Dataset

The project includes a synthetic facility IoT dataset containing approximately 10,000 records.

Main parameters:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Occupancy
- Water Consumption
- AQI
- Traffic Count
- Energy Consumption

## 🏗️ 3D Digital Twin

The platform provides an interactive 3D representation of a sustainable facility including:

- Buildings
- Roads
- Green Zones
- Solar Panels
- Water Infrastructure
- Parking
- IoT Sensors
- Facility Status
- Live KPIs
